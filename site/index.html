Bidik loop and GIF maker

Pick a screen recording from your device. This page looks for a stretch that plays as a seamless loop, lets you trim it by hand, and exports it as a looping GIF. It is the loop-and-GIF part of the Bidik Android app, re-written for the browser.

The video stays on your device: nothing is uploaded. It does not capture web pages or record the screen; that stays in the Android app, where it uses the phone's own WebView and screen-recording consent. This version is a separate JavaScript reimplementation of Bidik's loop search and GIF writer, tested on synthetic clips only. It is not verified to match the Android build, and neither one is validated on a real phone yet. GIFs are capped at 20 s, use a fixed 216-colour palette with dithering, and a long clip is analysed only for its first 80 s.

Use these when a status or navigation bar changes during the clip (a clock, for example), so it does not affect the match.
